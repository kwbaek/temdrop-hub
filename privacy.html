Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 · Service: TemDrop Science Lab (@temdroplab / @temdroplab_en)

1. Information We Collect

TemDrop Science Lab is a first-party creator publishing tool. We only store OAuth 2.0 access tokens, refresh tokens, and basic account identifiers (such as account ID and username) strictly for our own authorized brand accounts (@temdroplab and @temdroplab_en). We do not collect, sell, or store personal data from external viewers or third-party users.

2. How We Use Information

  • Authenticating authorized TemDrop channels via official platform OAuth 2.0 APIs (YouTube, Meta, Threads, TikTok).
  • Uploading original educational short-form videos and captions to authorized channels.

3. Data Storage & Security

OAuth credentials are stored locally on encrypted administrator hardware with restricted owner-only file permissions (0600) and are never shared with third parties.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Authorized account owners may revoke access at any time through each platform's security settings or request immediate credential deletion by emailing temdroplab@gmail.com.

5. Contact

Privacy inquiries and data deletion requests: temdroplab@gmail.com
